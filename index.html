The problem

Great ideas fail because building software is slow, expensive, and unclear.

No engineering capacity

Many founders have a strong idea and a clear market — but nobody on the team who can build it.

Agencies are slow and costly

Traditional agencies often mean long timelines and big budgets before you see anything that works.

Generic AI tools fall short

Off-the-shelf chatbots don’t understand your data, your customers, or the way your business actually runs.

The path is unclear

Without a technical background, it’s hard to know what to build first, what it costs, or where to start.

How we use Claude

Claude is part of how Tersa builds.

We use Claude at two levels: in how we build, and in what we build.

Inside our delivery process

How we work with clients, every engagement.

  • Research markets, competitors, and user needs.
  • Turn client goals into product requirements.
  • Draft product specifications and technical documentation.
  • Explore architecture and implementation options.
  • Accelerate prototyping and code review.
  • Improve quality through testing and documentation support.

Inside the products we build

What your users get, powered by the Claude API.

  • Build AI assistants and copilots.
  • Analyze documents and extract structured information.
  • Summarize long content.
  • Answer customer questions from a company knowledge base.
  • Automate repetitive business workflows.
  • Generate drafts, reports, and internal documentation.

We use Claude both to build better products for clients and to build AI-powered features into the products themselves.

  • People stay accountableEvery requirement, design decision, and line of code is reviewed by a person before it ships.
  • Grounded in your dataAI features answer from your own documents and knowledge base, with clear fallbacks to a human.
  • Privacy by designWe only share the client data a task needs, and agree with you up front on how it’s handled.

FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

What kinds of products does Tersa build?

Web applications, MVPs, dashboards, internal tools, AI assistants, and workflow automations. Most projects are web-based and focused on one core workflow done well.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with Tersa?

No. You bring knowledge of your customers and your business. We translate that into product decisions and technical choices, and explain them in plain language.

How does Tersa use Claude?

In two ways. In our delivery process, Claude helps with research, requirements, documentation, architecture exploration, prototyping, code review, and testing. In client products, we integrate the Claude API for features like assistants, document analysis, summarization, and workflow automation.

A person reviews all work before it ships.

Can Tersa add AI features to an existing product?

Yes. We start by reviewing your product and data to find where AI would actually help, then design and integrate the feature. Feasibility depends on your current stack, which we assess before committing to a scope.

What is the typical starting engagement?

Usually a focused product-discovery phase: we define the users, the first version’s scope, and a technical plan. From there, we agree on a build phase with clear milestones. Timelines and costs depend on scope, so we estimate them together after discovery.
