The problem

Most good ideas stall before anyone writes a line of code.

No one to build it

You know the market and the customer, but there’s no engineer on the team.

Agencies are slow and costly

A big agency can take months and a large budget before you see anything you can click.

Off-the-shelf AI doesn’t fit

A generic chatbot doesn’t know your data, your customers or how your business runs.

Hard to know where to start

Without a technical background, it’s hard to judge what to build first and what it should cost.

The solution

From a rough idea to software people can use.

We handle the product planning, design and development, and use Claude throughout so the work moves faster.

How we use Claude

We use Claude in two places.

In how we build your product, and inside the product itself.

Inside our delivery process

On every project.

  • Researching markets, competitors and user needs.
  • Turning your goals into product requirements.
  • Drafting specs and technical docs.
  • Comparing architecture and implementation options.
  • Faster prototyping and code review.
  • Writing tests and documentation.

Inside the products we build

Features your users see, built on the Claude API.

  • Assistants and copilots.
  • Reading documents and pulling out structured data.
  • Summaries of long content.
  • Answers to customer questions from your own help content.
  • Automated repetitive workflows.
  • Drafts of reports and internal docs.

We use Claude both to build better products for clients and to build AI-powered features into the products themselves.

  • A person signs offA person reviews every requirement, design decision and line of code before it ships.
  • Built on your dataAI features answer from your own documents and hand off to a person when they can’t.
  • Only the data we needWe only use the data a task needs, and agree with you up front on how it’s handled.

Process

How a project runs.

Four steps, an agreed scope at each one, and something real users can try at the end.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery call

    We talk through the idea, your goals, any constraints and what success looks like.

  2. Step 02

    Product definition

    We pin down the users, features, scope, where AI fits and how we’ll measure success.

  3. Step 03

    Prototype and build

    We build the MVP, internal tool or AI feature in short cycles, with regular check-ins.

  4. Step 04

    Launch and improve

    We deploy it, collect feedback from real users and keep improving it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What kinds of products does Tersa build?

Web apps, MVPs, dashboards, internal tools, AI assistants and workflow automations. Most projects are web-based and focus on doing one core workflow well.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with Tersa?

No. You know your customers and your business. We turn that into product and technical decisions and explain them in plain language.

How does Tersa use Claude?

Two ways. While we work, Claude helps with research, requirements, docs, architecture, prototyping, code review and testing. Inside client products, we use the Claude API for things like assistants, document analysis, summaries and automations.

A person reviews everything before it ships.

Can Tersa add AI features to an existing product?

Yes. First we look at your product and data to see where AI would help, then design and build the feature. What’s possible depends on your current stack, so we check that before agreeing on scope.

What is the typical starting engagement?

Usually a short discovery phase where we define the users, what the first version includes and a technical plan. Then we agree on a build phase with clear milestones. Time and cost depend on scope, so we estimate them together after discovery.
